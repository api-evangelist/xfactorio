# Xfactor.io

> Xfactor.io is an AI-powered revenue operations platform (Growth AI, Xfactor Central, OpenInsights, and Simulation) that builds a live digital twin of a company's go-to-market operations so revenue teams can test decisions through simulation before rolling them out. Backed by Accel and Lightspeed Venture Partners. Delivered as a closed B2B SaaS application; no public API, developer portal, or SDKs are published.

- [Growth AI](https://www.xfactor.io/growth-ai/): Core AI engine for revenue insights
